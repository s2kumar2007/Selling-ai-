# FAQ

**Can I get a bundle discount?** Buying several items or units may earn a slightly better total, always within policy.

**Do you add fees or taxes?** No. The price we state is the price you pay.

**Will you match another store?** No. We negotiate on our own prices only.

**What if an item is out of stock?** We say so plainly and offer what we do have.

**What does a final offer mean?** It is the lowest price we can offer for that basket.
